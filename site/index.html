Security & Audits

Institutional Rigor by Design

ClockLend was audited across 10 distinct re-audit passes with 103 deterministic on-chain test assertions.

✓ Pure Integer Fixed-Point Arithmetic

Zero floating-point math in the Solana SBF program. Prevents rounding exploits, IEEE-754 precision drift, and arithmetic underflow vulnerabilities.

✓ Isolated PDA Escrow Vaults

Funds never pool into single omnibus accounts. Every loan and pawn offer derives unique cryptographic Program Derived Addresses with front-run defense.

✓ Android Anti-Tamper Hardening

App refuses execution on rooted environments, Android emulators, or Frida/Xposed hooks. Protected by Android FLAG_SECURE against screen recording.

✓ Fail-Closed Oracle Staleness Guard

Feeds older than 600 seconds automatically halt new borrow transactions, protecting both liquidity providers and borrowers from flash market dislocations.
